Probes are split because a probe that crashes the renderer would take the whole report with it. Engine vintage and the JS/CSS feature checks are pure property lookups and cannot crash anything — start there, they answer the most important question. Everything that touches a subsystem this port may have stubbed is behind its own button.

Each button is independent. If one whites the screen, the results from the others are still in the report below, and the ones you already ran are kept in the address-bar hash.
